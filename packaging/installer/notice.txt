Lasso - screenshots with every editing tool on the frozen screen
https://sonela.app/lasso

WHAT IT INSTALLS
Lasso installs for you only (no administrator rights) into your user folder (AppData\Local\Lasso), with a Start menu entry and an uninstaller in Windows Settings > Apps. Your settings and library live in your user profile; pictures are saved in Pictures\Lasso.

PRIVACY
Lasso keeps your screenshots on this PC. It sends nothing anywhere unless you use a share-link feature, and then only the picture you chose to share.
